Privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026

In short

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. This page loads nothing from other services: fonts and styles come from the site itself. To show it to you, your browser connects to GitHub, which hosts the site and therefore sees your IP address.

Who is responsible

Pio Gino Steiner, Switzerland · info@piogino.ch
piogino.ch is a private, non-commercial website.

What happens when you visit

WhatProvider and locationWhat it receives
The website itself GitHub Pages: GitHub, Inc., USA (privacy statement) Your IP address, the page requested, browser details. GitHub logs these for operation and security.

Stored in your browser

This site sets no cookies. If you switch between light and dark mode, the choice is kept in your browser's local storage as preferred-theme. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it by clearing the site data in your browser's settings; the site works the same without it.

Contact by email

If you write to info@piogino.ch, your message and address are used only to answer you. The mailbox is hosted by Infomaniak in Switzerland.

The projects

The projects linked from the start page are separate websites. Each has its own privacy statement, linked at the bottom of its pages.

Data abroad

GitHub is based in the USA, so your IP address and the page request reach the USA when the site is loaded. GitHub states that it participates in the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; for details see its privacy statement linked above.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) you can ask what personal data about you is processed, and have it corrected or deleted. Write to info@piogino.ch. We keep no logs of visits to this site, so there is usually nothing we can link to you. You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Changes

This statement is updated when the site changes; the date at the top shows the current version.
